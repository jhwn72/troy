hey troy

What to text

Say it like you'd text a friend.

No app and no forms. A lot of answers are one tap, with a tapback or a poll. The messages below are examples.

Found something

Send a photo and where you found it. Troy logs it and texts you when the owner turns up.

Example
found these airpods at leavey
anything on it only the owner would know? that part stays between us

Lost something

Say what it is and where you last had it. Troy checks what's been found and asks you to describe it.

Example
lost my airpods at leavey
on it. checking what's been turned in

Free food

Leftovers after an event? Troy texts Trojans nearby who asked for food alerts. A tapback claims one.

What they get
yo acm is giving away free chipotle at leavey, about 3 bowls left
react if u want one

Something's broken

Troy logs it for the office that handles it and texts you when the status changes.

Example
the light is out on the 2nd floor of taper hall
reported to fms ✅ i'll text u when it moves.

Ask USG

Club funding, the LAX shuttle, thrift, dining hours. When Troy doesn't have this year's dates confirmed, he says so and points you to USG.

Example
how does my club get funding
recognized student orgs and usc undergrads can apply for usg funding on engagesc.

Your rules

Tell Troy how you want to be reached and he sticks to it.

Example
don't add me to group chats
got it. i won't add u to group chats

How it works

Three steps, then he's out of the way.

Troy connects you and steps back. The people and offices do the rest.

  1. Text a photo

    Or just say what's going on, in your own words.

  2. Troy matches and verifies

    He finds the likely person or office and runs a quick check first.

  3. He hands you off

    Once it checks out, Troy hands off and steps back.

The first time you text, Troy asks for your USC email to make sure you're a Trojan. Hackathon judges get a skip. That skip is for this demo only and wouldn't exist in a real campus version.

Trust

Careful by default.

Troy says he's an AI up front, and the rules below are written in code.

  • Quick check before any handoffAn owner has to describe the item before anything changes hands.
  • Contact info only when both say yesA group chat shares contact info, so Troy asks you both first. Say no and he passes messages along instead.
  • Dangerous items go to DPSTroy doesn't match those between students. He routes them to DPS.
  • Reply STOP anytimeOne word and Troy leaves you alone until you want to talk again.

Privacy. Troy only answers people who text him first. He keeps the last location you share so alerts are accurate, and other students never see it. Text “forget my location” to erase it, or “delete my data” to erase everything.

Troy is the front door. The offices stay in charge.
  • DPS Lost & Found
  • FMS
  • USC Housing maintenance
  • USG

Troy routes to these offices. In this prototype the office and committee handoffs are mocked, so nothing is filed with them, and none of these offices has reviewed or endorsed it.

Troy is a hackathon prototype built Nov 14 to 15, 2026 for the AI@USC Hackathon. It is not an official USC service.
