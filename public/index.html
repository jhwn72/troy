Why it feels different

  • Knows who's nearby.

    Troy only pings people close enough to act, so alerts stay useful instead of spammy.

  • Nothing to download.

    No app, no account page, no forms. Text a number and you're in.

  • Feels like texting a friend.

    Typing, reactions, live counts and group chats, all inside iMessage.

One engine behind every connection.

  1. Understand.

    Reads your text or photo and figures out what you need.

    Example: a photo of trays becomes "20 bowls of chipotle".

  2. Find.

    Looks for the right person or office by need, time and distance.

    Example: Trojans within a 15-minute walk.

  3. Check.

    Makes sure it's a real match before anyone is connected.

    Example: the owner describes the case before seeing the photo.

  4. Step 4

    Connect.

    Starts the group chat, sends the alert or routes it to the right office.

  5. Step 5

    Follow up.

    Texts you when something changes, so you never have to chase anyone.

Rides, lost items, free food, repairs and student concerns all run on these same five steps. New uses are just new connections.

Built to be safe from the first text.

  • Only Trojans.

    Every student verifies a USC email before Troy connects them to anyone.

  • Nothing shared without a yes.

    Phone numbers and names are only shared when both sides agree.

  • Your location stays yours.

    Troy keeps the last spot you share so walking times are right. It's never shown to another student, and 'forget my location' erases it.

  • You choose how you show up.

    Send concerns with your name or anonymously.

  • Checked before connected.

    Lost items are verified before a handoff. Dangerous items go straight to DPS.

  • No spam, ever.

    Only alerts close enough to matter, limits on everything else, and STOP works anytime. People who misuse it get removed.

FAQ

Do I need an app?

No. Troy lives in iMessage. Text the number and you're in.

How does Troy know I'm a student?

He asks for your USC email in the chat and sends you a code.

Who sees my info?

Only people you say yes to. Troy never shares your number or name without both sides agreeing.

Does Troy track my location?

Only if you share it. He keeps the last spot you shared to work out walking distance, never shows it to anyone, and 'forget my location' erases it.

Will I get spammed?

No. You only get alerts close enough to matter, and you choose what you hear about. Text STOP anytime.

Is this run by USC?

No. A student built it at a hackathon. It isn't an official USC service yet.
