Troy's pledge

What I keep, who sees it, and how to erase it.

  1. I

    I only answer people who text me first.

    A chat starts when you text "hey troy". I never text someone who hasn't opted in, and I can only see what you send to that chat.

  2. II

    I keep only what helps me help you.

    The messages in your chat with me, the notes and settings you give me, and the last location you share.

  3. III

    Your location is yours.

    While you are sharing it I keep it current so walking times are right. It is never shown to another student. Text "forget my location" and it is gone.

  4. IV

    Nothing about you is shared without your yes.

    Other students never see your name, number or messages unless you agree, each time.

  5. V

    Staff see only what you file.

    A report or a concern you send goes to the office that handles it. If you send a concern anonymously, your name does not go with it.

  6. VI

    You can leave whenever you want.

    Text "delete my data" to erase your messages, notes, profile and location. Text STOP and I leave you alone until you want to talk again.

  7. VII

    I will be honest about what I am.

    I am an AI. An outside AI service processes messages so I can write replies. I am a hackathon prototype built by a USC student, not an official USC service, and for this demo I run on his Mac.
