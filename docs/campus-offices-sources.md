# Campus office directory sources

Fetched and re-verified 2026-11-14 from official USC pages. Each fact below was seen on the cited page by a researcher and again by a separate fact-checker.

## USC Counseling & Mental Health Services
- Page names Counseling & Mental Health Services under USC Student Health; contact (213) 740-9355 (WELL); MySHR at https://usc.edu/myshr is used to book a workshop, sign up for a therapy group, book Let's Talk or request a therapist appointment; 'These services are provided for through the USC Student Health Fee (SHF)'; link labelled 'Crisis support, 24/7' (https://sites.usc.edu/counselingandmentalhealth/)
- Crisis support page shows '24/7 line: 213-740-9355'; for thoughts or behavior of self-harm, call to see a therapist on triage in the Engemann and Eric Cohen Student Health Centers. No 988 or text line number is printed on the page. (https://sites.usc.edu/counselingandmentalhealth/crisis-support/)

## USC Student Basic Needs
- Office name Student Basic Needs; address 1025 W 34th Street, King Hall KOH STE 103, Los Angeles, CA 90089; email basicneeds@usc.edu; menu lists Food Support, Housing Support, Economic Support, Tommy's Closet, Resource Navigation & Partnerships; also highlights Student Basic Needs Supplemental Funding. No phone or office hours on the page. (https://studentbasicneeds.usc.edu/)
- Trojan Food Pantry operates out of King Hall (KOH), Suite 103 on UPC; fall and spring Tuesdays, Wednesdays and Thursdays 10:00 AM to 2:00 PM, about 15 weeks from the first week of classes through Classes End week; summer Wednesdays 11:00 AM to 2:00 PM, first week of June through last week of July; intake form required once per semester (screenshot shown at first visit each term); physical USCard required at every visit; bring a reusable bag; eligibility: enrolled, no active dining hall meal plan (20 or fewer remaining meals may be considered), experiencing food insecurity (https://studentbasicneeds.usc.edu/programs-and-services/food-support/trojan-food-pantry/)
- Food support programs listed: Trojan Food Pantry, CalFresh, Emergency Meal Swipe Program (meal swipes on USCard for residential dining halls), Tommy's Table (surplus food from private campus events, students alerted, first come first served) (https://studentbasicneeds.usc.edu/programs-and-services/food-support/)
- Tommy's Closet is a Student Basic Needs program that collects lightly used clothing donations and gives them to students at no cost (https://studentbasicneeds.usc.edu/programs-and-services/tommys-closet/)

## Campus Support & Intervention (CSI)
- Campus Support & Intervention; phone 213-740-0411; email uscsupport@usc.edu, 'A member of our team will respond to emails within one business day'; Tutor Campus Center, TCC Suite 421, Los Angeles, CA 90089; 'we are not an emergency resource and are not available 24/7'; 'In case of an emergency, please contact the Department of Public Safety at 213-740-4321'; services include navigation of complex issues, resource referrals and recommendations, Student Health Leave of Absence, professor notifications case by case. No office hours listed. (https://campussupport.usc.edu/)
- Page 'Trojans Care 4 Trojans (TC4T)' loads and describes a private and anonymous request form for Trojans to help a member of the Trojan community; the form itself is hosted off usc.edu at uscit.tfaforms.net (https://campussupport.usc.edu/tc4t/)

## USC Lyft Rides Program
- USC Lyft Rides Program; 'Current USC students, faculty and staff have access to free rides provided by Lyft'; UPC 7:00pm to 2:00am seven days a week; HSC 5:00pm to 2:00am Monday-Friday only, including service to Union Station; on home football game days (fall only) the program begins 5 hours after scheduled kickoff or normal program hours, whichever is later; choose Shared; ride must start and end within the campus geofence; rides with added stops not covered; pass reads 'USC Lyft Rides' or 'HSC USC Rides' under Payment; Lyft Invite form at https://transnet.usc.edu/index.php/lyft-invite/ if no pass; UPC area east of Normandie Ave, south of Washington Blvd, west of Main St, north of Martin Luther King Jr. Blvd; contact auxtrans@usc.edu, (213) 740-3575 (https://transnet.usc.edu/index.php/how-to-use-lyft/)
- The alternate URL also loads, is titled USC Lyft Rides Program and shows the same UPC and HSC hours (https://transnet.usc.edu/index.php/campus-cruiser-program/how-to-use-lyft/)

## Office of Academic Records and Registrar
- Office name is Office of Academic Records and Registrar; home page links to Registration Services, Transcripts Services (https://arr.usc.edu/transcripts-services/), Verification (https://arr.usc.edu/verification/) and Registration Calendar pages (https://arr.usc.edu/)
- University Park Campus office at 700 Childs Way, John Hubbard Hall Lobby (JHH), Los Angeles, CA 90089-0912; Hours: 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM, Monday - Friday; no general email, general inquiries go to the ask.usc.edu contact form (https://arr.usc.edu/contact-us/)
- Registration Services (213) 740-8500 handles registration errors, Web Registration and OASIS help, registration questions; Grades, Transcripts and Verifications (213) 740-7445, JHH B10, handles transcripts and enrollment verification for current students and alumni (https://arr.usc.edu/contact-us/)
- Registration Calendar page lists add/drop dates per term and links Web Registration at https://webreg.usc.edu/Login (loads, HTTP 200) and the Schedule of Classes at https://classes.usc.edu/home (https://arr.usc.edu/registration-calendar/)
- Transcripts Services page loads and explains how to order official transcripts (current students through Experience USC, former students through Parchment) (https://arr.usc.edu/transcripts-services/)

## USC Office of International Services (OIS)
- Office name USC Office of International Services; 649 W. 34th St., Royal Street Parking Structure, Suite 101, Los Angeles, CA 90089-1626; general and F-1 students use the askOIS contact form linked at /ask-ois/; J-1 students j1student@usc.edu, J-1 scholars j1scholar@usc.edu; no general office phone or office hours listed (https://ois.usc.edu/contact/)
- Drop-in advising via Zoom: F-1 Monday through Friday from 1 to 3 p.m.; J-1 Tuesdays and Wednesdays from 1 to 3 p.m.; Zoom room access closes at 2:30 p.m.; OIS is closed on all university-observed holidays and during official university closures (https://ois.usc.edu/contact/)
- "International F-1 and J-1 students are required to enroll in and complete a full course load each semester"; "Separate OIS approval is still required before dropping below full-time enrollment." (https://ois.usc.edu/maintaining-status/reduced-course-load/)
- Home page serves students, J-1 scholars and dependents, offers help "from travel planning to immigration requirements", and links pages on student visa types, applying for a visa, OPT, CPT and travel/reentry (https://ois.usc.edu/)

## USCard Services
- Office name USCard Services; 'If your card is lost or stolen, log in to your account to freeze your card'; Log In link https://mycard.usc.edu/login/ (https://mycard.usc.edu/)
- Login page 'Log In to Your USCard Account'; after login you can 'Freeze card if lost or stolen' and 'Reactivate card if found' (https://mycard.usc.edu/login/)
- Office hours Monday to Friday 9 am to 5 pm; University Park Campus Customer Service Center, McCarthy Way Parking Structure, 620 McCarthy Way, Los Angeles, CA 90089-1336, (213) 740-8709, uscard@usc.edu; HSC One Stop, Seaver Residence Hall, 1969 Zonal Avenue, (323) 442-2110 (https://mycard.usc.edu/contact/)
- 'Replacements are $25.'; REPLACEMENT ID link points to https://mycard.usc.edu/replacement-id; old ID cannot be reactivated once a replacement is processed; must surrender current ID if you have it (https://mycard.usc.edu/get-your-uscard/)
- Replacement ID page loads; explains the process and links to two online request forms (use photo on file, or upload a new photo); does not state the fee itself (https://mycard.usc.edu/replacement-id)
